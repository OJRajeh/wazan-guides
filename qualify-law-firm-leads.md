# How to qualify a law firm lead before the first call

A typical contact form collects a name, an email and a short message. Someone at the firm then has to call back and ask everything else. That first call becomes a data-collection call, and it often happens before anyone knows whether the matter is a fit.

Qualifying a lead at intake moves those questions to the form, so the first conversation can be about the matter.

## Start with what every lead needs

Every practice area needs a few basics:

- Name and the best way to reach the person
- How they found the firm (this feeds your referral reporting later)
- The type of matter, in the person's own words
- Where the matter is, since location often decides whether you can take it
- How urgent it is, including any hard deadline

## Add questions specific to the practice area

A single generic form cannot ask what a personal injury intake and a family law intake each need. Build one short branch per practice area. For example:

- **Personal injury:** date of the incident, whether the person has seen a doctor, whether another firm is already involved.
- **Family law:** whether there is an existing court order, whether children are involved, whether the other party has a lawyer.
- **Immigration:** current status, any upcoming deadline, country of citizenship.
- **Estate planning:** whether documents already exist, and what changed that prompted the inquiry.

Keep each branch to the questions your attorney would otherwise ask in the first two minutes of the call.

## Decide what counts as qualified

Write the rule down before you build the form. Common conditions are matter type you handle, a location you serve, no conflict with an existing client, and a timeline you can meet. A lead that fails a condition can get a polite referral message instead of a callback slot.

## Put the answers where your team already works

Qualification only helps if the answers land in the tool your firm uses. Ideally, fields go straight into your practice-management system, or into a spreadsheet if that is what you run today. Retyping answers from an email defeats the purpose.

## Record the source on every lead

Add the referral source as a required field with a fixed list of options, not free text. Free text produces "google", "Google", "found you on google" and "web", and the report you build later is useless.

## What to review monthly

Look at how many leads failed qualification and why, and whether the questions you added changed how many leads turned into matters. Remove any question nobody uses.
