# What a free law firm process audit should cover

A process audit looks at how your practice actually runs, names the core problem and puts a fix next to it. Before you commit money to software or consultants, it should tell you where time is being lost.

## What a good audit looks at

- **Intake.** How leads arrive, what is asked, who follows up and how long it takes.
- **Matter opening.** How many times the same details are entered, and into how many systems.
- **Reporting.** Whether anyone can say, today, where leads come from and why some are lost.
- **Software spend.** What you pay for compared with what the team uses. See [how to audit software spend](/wazan-guides/law-firm-software-spend-audit/).
- **Handoffs.** Places where work waits on one person.

## What you should receive

A short written summary that names the main problem in plain language, shows the proposed fix next to it, and says what would change. It should not require you to sign anything to read it.

## Questions to ask any provider

1. Is the audit really free, and what happens if I do not hire you?
2. Will you work around the tools I already use?
3. Who does the work, and who do I contact when something breaks?
4. How will we know it worked? Ask for one number to measure before and after.

## How to prepare

Gather your intake form, a list of the software you pay for, and one example of a matter from first contact to open. Thirty minutes with the person who handles intake is often enough.
